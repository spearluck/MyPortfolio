Who I am

About me

I'm Vincent Giannone, a developer based in South Africa with a passion for building real, functional web applications from the ground up.

I enjoy the full stack — from designing databases and writing backend logic to crafting clean, responsive interfaces that people actually want to use.

FurniBid is my most recent project — a fully deployed furniture marketplace built with Django, PostgreSQL, and Cloudinary, complete with user auth, messaging, image uploads and live search filters.

I'm always looking to build something new and improve my craft.
